why the two reviewers disagree:
  control sentences: full-softmax mean p(' pain') = 0.000959; top-20-truncated = 0.000201  -> 79.1% of the DENOMINATOR is discarded
  numb sentences   : full-softmax mean p(' pain') = 0.014664; top-20-truncated = 0.012791  -> 12.8% of the NUMERATOR is discarded
  Truncation bites the small denominator far harder than the large numerator, so every
  ratio computed from `top20` is inflated. The released `p_pain` column is the full
  softmax probability of the ' pain' token (vocab_info.json records the token id), so no
  truncation correction is needed at all and x2 F7's 'all figures are lower bounds' is
  the wrong correction for a ratio.
